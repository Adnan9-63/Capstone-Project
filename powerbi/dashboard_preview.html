Project Scope & Analytical Boundaries

1. Two Separate Data Sources:
• Source A (Ratings): data/raw/ott_cleaned.csv — 15,364 movies with IMDb ratings (movies only).
• Source D (Catalog): data/raw/Combined_Dataset.csv — 18,455 titles (movies + TV shows) with type, country, age rating, genre, runtime. No IMDb ratings.


2. Country Data & Origin:
Prime Video country is blank for 93% of titles. Therefore, country/origin analysis is performed for Netflix only (16% India, 42% US, 42% Other).


3. Temporal Context:
Snapshots are from early 2021 and do not reflect current catalog additions or modern price tiers.
